Master of Philosophy (MPhil)

DWU Postgraduate & Research Centre

Program Overview

The Master of Philosophy (MPhil) program is completed by a research thesis of about 40,000 words on an approved topic under the guidance of a supervisory team. The candidate demonstrates the capacity to carry out independent research.

The MPhil program is offered as a stand-alone degree serving as a stepping stone to the PhD degree. It is an internationally recognized award of a higher level of supervised scholarship at master's level.

The MPhil program culminates with external examination of the thesis, which must be passed for the candidate to be eligible for the award. The normal period of candidature is two years full-time or four years part-time.

Purpose

The MPhil Program aims to produce scholars and scientists able to conduct research autonomously and contribute to the National Research Agenda. The MPhil award by thesis alone is recommended for those in, or aiming to enter, an academic position or a professional research post.

Facilities

Divine Word University offers excellent, modern, residential, teaching and ICT facilities in a beautiful, friendly and peaceful environment. Higher Degree students have the dedicated Moramora Laboratory in the DWU Postgraduate and Research Centre in which to work.

Admission Requirements

Applicants will normally have completed an undergraduate degree with an average grade of Distinction or higher. English writing skills are essential and applicants may be asked to demonstrate these before being accepted.

Costs

  • K100 non-refundable application fee.
  • K10,000 per year for tuition, internet access when on campus and supervisory services.
  • Current fee for on-campus accommodation and meals is K2,800 per fortnight (K200 per day).
